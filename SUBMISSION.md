# MatchRef — Decentralized Esports Tournament Dispute Resolution

MatchRef gives tournament organizers and players a shared record of disputed results and penalties. The organizer locks the rule, player addresses and a commit-pinned official match report. Players file and respond within on-chain deadlines. GenLayer validators independently fetch and hash the report, interpret the locked rule and record a consensus recommendation. The organizer records whether they adopt or override it, or close without action, along with a rationale. Try the fictional server-crash case `night-circuit-042` in the review desk. Use Studio’s built-in account to sign a new case and paste the transaction hash to track finality. The public case record includes the locked rule, arguments, recommendation and receipt hash.
